Living
Live independently. Belong by choice.

A home of your own, with more around you when you want it.

YOOBELONG is designed for people who want to remain independent while making everyday life easier, more social and more connected. Start with your own apartment, then add community, local activities and practical services on your terms.

Independent living, not institutional living

Your apartment remains your private space. YOOBELONG adds an age-friendly living environment and a digital community layer around it, without making care the center of everyday life.

Senior living can mean very different things. Some people are looking for a more accessible apartment. Others want a lively community, practical help nearby or the reassurance that additional support can be organized later. YOOBELONG brings these needs into one connected living experience.

Common questions

Before choosing a senior-living apartment.

Do I have my own apartment?

Yes. The YOOBELONG concept is centered on private apartments rather than institutional accommodation.

Do I have to use services?

No. Services are designed to be optional and selected according to individual needs and the local offering.

What happens if no apartment is available?

You can register your interest or join a waiting list for a residence and preferred apartment profile.

Is YOOBELONG a nursing-care provider?

No. The portal focuses on independent living, community and access to services. Care arrangements depend on the residence and external providers.

Can family members help with the search?

Yes. The housing journey can be used by prospective residents as well as relatives helping them compare suitable options.

Can I see what is available in my city?

Yes. City pages lead to local apartment catalogs, districts, residences and relevant community information.
